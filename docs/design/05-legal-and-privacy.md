# Legal applicability, licences and privacy

Research checked on **1 October 2026** against the linked official sources. This is a scoped design assessment, not a legal opinion or universal compliance certification. The operator is in **Gujarat, India**, actively targeting the US, Canada, Mexico, China, India and Europe including the UK, with future B2B and B2C subscriptions. That scope comes from the owner, not their device location.

Public statistical data may not identify individuals, but IP addresses, security logs, support messages, account records and third-party requests can be personal data. A public site can therefore have privacy duties before accounts or tracking exist. B2B use does not remove protection for identifiable people working at companies.

Store security logs apart from readership reports. Restrict access to named administrators, audit reads and never repurpose logs for ad profiling. Aggregation is not anonymous if IPs/IDs can still be recovered. Backups need expiry and a deletion ledger so a restoration does not resurrect deleted account data. Legal holds are scoped, justified and access-restricted.

Privacy-request procedure: publish a working contact; acknowledge promptly; verify identity proportionately; determine jurisdiction, records and deadline; search the operator and processors; respond through a secure channel; record outcome and exceptions without excessive identity evidence. Apply the shortest applicable statutory clock rather than one invented worldwide deadline. Do not demand a passport for a simple mailing-list deletion. Future dashboard export/deletion must complement, not replace, accessible contact support.

## Draft notices and publication gates

The following is **unpublished template text**, not a representation of a live site. Replace brackets only with verified facts; remove inapplicable statements after a network/configuration audit. Localize where required. No draft should be deployed with unresolved placeholders.

## Specific professional reviews before launch

1. **Indian counsel, before public commercial launch:** entity/form/address, SPDI/DPDP transition, CERT-In incident/log scope, current retention rules and future DPDP security-log obligations, grievance responsibility, accessibility and consumer disclosures. A CA separately determines Gujarat GST/export and invoicing treatment.
2. **EU/UK privacy/consumer counsel, before actively targeting those users with processing:** representative duties, processor/transfer arrangements, log lawful basis, country-specific notices and consent. Before billing, withdrawal, renewal/cancellation, accessibility, VAT and the 2026/2027 changes need review.
3. **China counsel, before active China targeting/collection:** extraterritorial PIPL, local representative/filing, cross-border pathways and network-data rules. Separately assess consumer billing, tax, localization, reachability and any mainland hosting/ICP plans. An overseas static site is not automatically outside these duties.
4. **Mexico and Canada counsel, before relevant marketing/billing:** Mexico’s new privacy framework and December 2025 renewal changes; Canadian federal/provincial/privacy, Quebec language/contract and CASL rules; local tax professional for destination taxes.
5. **US counsel, before programmatic ads or consumer billing:** current state thresholds, tracking/sale-share characterization, renewal/cancellation notices and accessible operation; no reliance on the vacated federal click-to-cancel rule.
6. **Source/licence reviewer, before adding datasets/creative assets:** exact permission to commercially reuse and redistribute every new source, classification supplement, logo/image, dependency and sponsor creative.

The current package verifies the named official materials and identifies required actions. It does not resolve every local law, execute provider contracts, perform tax registration or prove a deployed system complies. Launch-country gates must remain visible in the release checklist; a global business ambition is not evidence that every market is ready for paid service.
